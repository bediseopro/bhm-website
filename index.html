FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Does it edit my blog?
Only the fixes you preview and approve. You see every AI-generated change before it's applied, can regenerate any suggestion, and nothing goes live silently. It only ever edits blog content — never your theme, product pages, or storefront.
Do I need Google Analytics for this to work?
No. Blog Health Monitor reads Google Search Console, which is the source Google itself uses for search performance. You connect it in one click — no Analytics setup required.
What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?
Both plans run every check on every post, every week. Pro includes 10 AI fixes a month — ideal for staying on top of a healthy blog. Pro+ includes 500 fixes a month, enough to clear a large blog's entire backlog and keep it clean.
My blog has hundreds of posts. Can it handle that?
Yes — scanning is unlimited on both plans, and results are sorted by impact so the worst posts surface first. A 500+ post blog scans in about a minute.
Will it slow down my store?
No. The app runs inside your Shopify admin and adds nothing to your live storefront, so there's zero impact on page speed for your shoppers.
How do I cancel?
In one click, anytime, from your Shopify admin. There's a 14-day free trial, and billing runs through Shopify — so you're never locked in.
