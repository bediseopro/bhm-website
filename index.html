The problem

Content decay is silent. By the time you notice, the traffic is gone.

  • Rankings slip a position at a time. A post drops from #4 to #7 and loses half its clicks — with no error, no alert, nothing in your Shopify dashboard.
  • Nobody re-checks old posts. You published it, it ranked, you moved on. Google kept re-evaluating it every day since.
  • Search Console has the answer — buried. The data exists, but comparing every page across time windows by hand takes hours you don't have.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Do I need Google Analytics for this to work?

No. Blog Health Monitor uses Google Search Console, which is free and focused on exactly one thing: how your pages perform in Google Search. If your store isn't on Search Console yet, setting it up takes about ten minutes and we point you to Google's official guide.

What data does the app access?

Two things: your blog posts (via Shopify, to check links and content) and your Search Console performance data (read-only — clicks, impressions, and positions for your own pages). We never modify your site, never access customer data, and never share or sell any data. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Will it slow down my store?

No. The app runs entirely outside your storefront — nothing is added to your theme, no scripts load for your visitors. Scans run on our servers against Shopify's and Google's APIs.

My blog has hundreds of posts. Can it handle that?

Yes — every scan covers your entire blog, whether that's 10 posts or 1,000. Weekly scans run automatically in the background, so size never becomes your problem.

What do I do when a post is flagged?

Each alert includes the likely cause, which tells you the fix: a ranking slip usually calls for a content refresh, a money leak means adding relevant product links, broken links need updating or removing, and thin posts should be expanded or merged. You stay in control — the app diagnoses, you decide.

How do I cancel?

Uninstall the app from your Shopify admin at any time — billing stops immediately through Shopify, and your data is deleted from our systems. No emails to send, no hoops.
