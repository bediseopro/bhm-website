The problem

Content decay is silent. By the time you notice, the traffic is gone.

Rankings slip a position at a time. A post drops from #4 to #7 and loses half its clicks — with no error, no alert, nothing in your Shopify dashboard.

Nobody re-checks old posts. You published it, it ranked, you moved on. Google kept re-evaluating it every day since — and links inside it kept dying.

Search Console has the answer — buried. The data exists, but comparing every page across time windows by hand takes hours you don't have.

Why it's different

Other tools show you a number went down. This one tells you why.

  • Cause diagnosis, not just alerts. Every decaying page is labelled: Ranking slip (Google demoted you), Demand drop (fewer people searching), or Mixed — because the fix is different for each.
  • Drill into the exact search queries. Click any page to see which keywords lost clicks, with position and impression changes — so you know precisely what to rewrite.
  • Fair comparisons by design. 28-day windows align weekdays and skip Google's unfinished recent data — so a weekend or a data lag never triggers a false alarm.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Do I need Google Analytics for this to work? +

No. The app uses Google Search Console — the free tool where Google reports your actual search clicks, impressions, and positions. If your site is verified in Search Console, you're ready. If it isn't yet, verification takes about five minutes and we point you to Google's guide.

What data does the app access? +

Two things, both read-only: your blog posts through Shopify (to check words, links, and images) and your search performance reports through Search Console (to detect traffic decay). It never touches your theme, your products, your customers, or your orders — and it can never edit anything. Full details in our privacy policy.

Will it slow down my store? +

No. The app adds zero code to your storefront — no scripts, no theme changes, no pixels. All scanning happens on our servers, completely outside your shop, so your page speed is untouched.

My blog has hundreds of posts. Can it handle that? +

Yes — it's built for exactly that. Scans routinely analyze 1,000+ pages, results are filterable by issue type with the worst posts sorted first, and a 100-post blog scans in under a minute.

What do I do when a post is flagged? +

The flag tells you the fix. Ranking slip → refresh the content and internal links. Demand drop → the topic is fading; consider retargeting the post. Money leak → add product links. Broken link or image → repair or remove it. Thin content → expand or merge it. You always know why a post was flagged, not just that it was.

How do I cancel? +

Uninstall the app from your Shopify admin — one click. Billing stops immediately through Shopify, and your data is deleted from our systems automatically. No emails to send, no retention hoops.
