Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026 · Last updated: July 11, 2026

The short version

Blog Health Monitor reads two things: your store's blog posts (through Shopify) and your own site's search performance data (through Google Search Console, read-only). We use that data for exactly one purpose — showing you the health reports and alerts you signed up for. We don't sell data, we don't share it with advertisers, and when you uninstall the app, we delete it.

1. Who we are

Blog Health Monitor ("the app", "we", "us") is a Shopify application operated by Bedi SEO that monitors the health of a store's blog content: declining Google search traffic, posts without product links, broken links, and thin content. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what rights you have. It applies to merchants who install the app and to visitors of this website.

Contact for anything in this policy: bediseopro@gmail.com.

3. Google user data

When you click "Connect Google Search Console", you sign in with Google and grant the app read-only access to your Search Console data. We use this access solely to retrieve search performance reports (clicks, impressions, and positions per page) for the site property you select, so the app can detect traffic decay on your blog pages.

Limited Use disclosure. Blog Health Monitor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to third parties except as needed to run the app's own infrastructure (see section 5) or as required by law. Humans do not read your Google data except with your permission for support, for security purposes, or where required by law.

You can disconnect Google at any time — from inside the app, or by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further collection immediately.

4. How we use data

  • To run weekly and on-demand scans of your blog and produce the health reports shown in the app.
  • To send you a weekly email alert when the scan finds pages that need attention.
  • To maintain scan history so the app can compare time periods and show trends.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, do not share it with data brokers, and do not use Google user data to train machine-learning or AI models.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

The app runs on trusted infrastructure providers that act as data processors on our behalf:

  • Railway — hosts the application and its database, where store details, blog scan results, and Search Console report data are stored.
  • Resend — delivers the weekly alert emails to the address you choose.
  • Shopify and Google — the platforms you connect, governed by their own privacy policies.

All connections use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access credentials such as Google tokens are stored securely and used only by the app's automated scans.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • While the app is installed: we keep your scan history so trends can be shown over time.
  • When you uninstall: your store's data — including stored Google Search Console data and tokens — is deleted from our systems within 30 days, in line with Shopify's mandatory data-removal requirements.
  • On request: you can ask for earlier deletion, or a copy of the data we hold about your store, by emailing bediseopro@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you are located (including under the GDPR in Europe and similar laws elsewhere), you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email bediseopro@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Cookies

The app itself uses only the session cookies required to keep you signed in inside your Shopify admin. This marketing website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

The app is a business tool for store owners and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you by email or inside the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
